# Closing Plays (learned)

## seller shares hardship or loss
**When:** thank you so much ms [name], I'm so sorry to hear that, I'm here for you 200% if you ever need to talk or anything we are here to help
**Play:** shows you care about them as a person, not just the deal, builds trust when they're vulnerable

## seller sends photos or info
**When:** thank you so much ms [name], and again thank you for being helpful and making things easier for us so we can make it easy for you
**Play:** gratitude boomerang—appreciate their effort and tie it to mutual benefit

## communication breaks down on both sides
**When:** thank you so much ms [name] is crazy because we were doing the same I think sometimes it be your connection and then the office, if you're free today I can assure yahjair call you so we can move forward no more scaring each other
**Play:** own your side, blame tech not people, inject warmth (no more scaring each other), pivot to call

## seller agrees to something or says ok
**When:** thank you so much and yes that's ok I will hear from you soon thank you | have a blessed day
**Play:** double thank-you bookends, confirm you heard them, close with blessing, keep it moving without lingering

## you've been quiet or MIA
**When:** hey ms [name] it's been so many problems back to back in the office I'm sorry for going mia, we will be reaching out within the next couple of days for further discussion
**Play:** apologize plainly, give honest reason, commit to timeline, no excuses just ownership

## seller is responsive but hasn't jumped on call yet
**When:** good afternoon ms [name] I'm just checking in making sure everything is ok on your end, we are still preparing a deal on our end
**Play:** check their well-being first, reassure deal is alive, no pressure just presence
