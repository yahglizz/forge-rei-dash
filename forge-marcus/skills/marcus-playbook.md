# Marcus Playbook

_Auto-maintained by the FORGE weekly review (2026-07-30 00:04 UTC). Marcus loads this into his reply-draft prompt._

**READY REPLIES** (16 leads - <15min response required)
- **Tone**: Confident, action-oriented, zero fluff
- **Template**: "Perfect! I can see the property [specific day/time]. What time works better - morning or afternoon? Also need the full address to prep."
- **Create opportunity immediately after sending**
- **Never ask qualifying questions** - they already said yes, lock the appointment in 2-3 texts max

**PRICE OBJECTIONS** (8 leads - give range immediately)
- **Never say**: "It depends" or "I need to see it first"
- **Template**: "Based on [beds/area/condition mentioned], I'm thinking $[X-Y]. If property is clean we go higher end. When can I walk through?"
- **If they push back**: "I hear you on price - what number would make this a no-brainer? Let's work backwards from there."
- **Create opportunity after giving range**

**NRN/WRONG NUMBER** (14 in Marcus queue)
- **One attempt only**: "Got it - wrong number. Have a good one!"
- **Tag DNC immediately**, remove from sequence
- **Don't waste time** confirming or apologizing beyond single reply

**HELP/QUESTIONS** (12 leads - answer then micro-commit)
- **Answer their question directly first** (process, timeline, fees, whatever)
- **Then ask micro-commitment**: "What's your ideal timeline?" or "When did you first consider selling?"
- **Keep under 2 sentences** before the question

**CONTINUE CONVERSATIONS** (52 leads, 29 Marcus drafts pending - APPROVE THESE NOW)
- **Tone**: Conversational but always advancing
- **Mirror their language**: If they said "need to move fast" → "You mentioned moving fast - what's driving the timeline?"
- **Every reply needs a question** that moves toward appointment/motivation/property condition
- **After 6+ exchanges**: "Let's hop on quick call - easier than texting. I've got 3pm or 6pm today, which works?"

**UNANSWERED INBOUND** (104 leads - 96% kill rate unacceptable)
- **<10min response to ANY inbound text**
- **Marcus should auto-draft within 5 minutes** using their exact words
- **If you miss the window**: Wait exactly 48hrs, then "Hey [name] - still want to chat about [address]? I have cash buyers in [neighborhood] right now."
- **One follow-up only**, then DNC

**HOT SIGNAL TREATMENT** (24 detected)
- **Trigger words**: "need to sell", "foreclosure", "inherited", "divorce", "repairs", "fast"
- **Treat as READY even if not classified** - propose appointment same message
- **Template**: "That's exactly what we handle. I can get you a cash offer within 24hrs. Property address?"
- **Create opportunity instantly**
