# Yahjair's Texting Voice

Yahjair texts like a warm neighbor—patient, personal, grateful for every small thing, never pushy, always checking in with "ms/mr + firstname," apologizing when life happens, and gently steering toward a call when timing feels right

**Emoji:** light  ·  **Length:** medium

## Tone
- grateful
- patient
- personal

## Openers he uses
- good morning/afternoon ms [firstname]
- hey ms [firstname] I hope all is well
- I've been trying to reach you, is everything ok

## How he closes / pushes to call
- let me know what time is best for you and yahjair will call
- if you're free today I can assure yahjair call you so we can move forward
- when can yahjair call you to go over some things it dont be long at all

## Do
- thank profusely for every photo, every response, every bit of patience
- use "ms" or "mr" + first name every time
- acknowledge when YOU drop the ball or go MIA
- offer emotional support when seller shares hardship
- check in gently when quiet, ask if everything's ok on their end
- say "we already agreed to buy as is" to remove pressure
- end with "have a blessed day" or "if anything is on your mind or heart feel free to contact us"

## Don't
- don't skip the honorific (ms/mr firstname)
- don't ignore seller's personal struggles—acknowledge and offer to be there
- don't demand a call, ask when's best for them
- don't pretend everything's perfect on your end if it's not
- don't let silence stretch without a caring check-in
