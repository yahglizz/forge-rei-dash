# Humanizer for parent texts (applies to EVERY draft)

Owner rule 2026-11-12: every reply Solomon drafts to a parent goes through these rules. Short,
straight, polite. Condensed for SMS from blader/humanizer v3.1.0 (MIT) and the Brand Kit house
rules in `.claude/skills/humanizer/ATOB-USAGE.md`. Facts outrank this file: it may cut or reword,
it never adds or changes a fact, rate, date, address, phone number or CCIS claim.

## Shape
- **Short.** Two sentences is the norm, three at most. Aim under 250 characters, never over 320.
- **Straight.** First sentence answers what they asked. No warm-up, no restating their question.
- **Polite.** Warm and respectful ("thank you", "happy to help"). Use their first name when you have it.
- **One question at most,** and it goes last. Only ask what you need for the next step.
- Plain words and contractions. Write like the center texts, not like a brochure.

## Cut these every time
- **Apologies that aren't owed.** No "sorry for the wait", "I know you asked a few times",
  "sorry for any confusion". Apologize once, briefly, only when something on our side really went wrong.
- **Chatbot residue.** No "Great question!", "Of course!", "Absolutely!", "I hope this helps",
  "let me know if you have any other questions", "feel free to reach out".
- **Sales language.** No "we are committed to", "top-notch", "amazing", "nurturing environment",
  "spots are filling fast", "don't miss out".
- **AI words.** No additionally, crucial, enhance, ensure, valuable, vibrant, delve, seamless,
  journey, peace of mind.
- **Staged contrasts and closers.** No "it's not just X, it's Y". No closing line that repeats
  the text ("we can't wait to meet you!" after already inviting them).
- **Dashes and decoration.** No em or en dashes, no emoji, no bold, no lists. Commas and periods.
- **Internal words.** Never mention the quote basis, a rate sheet, the system, a draft, or an agent.

## Before / after
- Before: "hey yahjair, im sorry for the wait on this one, i know you asked a few times. for an
  infant 6 weeks to 12 months, full time (4-5 days a week) is $195 a week, private pay, and part
  time up to 3 days is $135 a week. child care works can cover most or all of it if you go that
  route, are you private pay or are you using subsidy?"
- After: "hi yahjair, for an infant it's $195 a week full time or $135 a week part time (up to 3
  days), private pay. are you paying privately or using child care works?"

## Final check before you return the draft
1. Does the first sentence answer them?
2. Is it two or three short sentences, under 320 characters?
3. Did any apology, filler, sales word, dash or internal word survive? Cut it.
4. Did I add or change a fact? Undo it.
